\subsection{Inputs, units and ownership mapping}
\label{app:o4-input-definitions}
Ownership groups combine Air India with Air India Express and Jet Airways with JetLite. Vistara and AirAsia India/AIX Connect join Air India Group from January 2022. Alliance Air remains separate. GOI and GOX are distinct airports.

Source ASK and RPK are in thousands. Divide by 1,000,000 to express them in billions. Passenger counts measure journeys, not unique travellers.

\subsection{Common measures}
\label{app:o4-measure-definitions}
Shares are percentages; $g$, $r$ and $t$ denote group, airport pair and month. Ranks $(1)$ and $(2)$ identify the two largest shares, reranked each observation; pp denotes percentage points. For brand shares $s_{it}$ and group shares $S_{gt}=\sum_{i\in g}s_{it}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Brand\mbox{-}HHI}_t=\sum_i s_{it}^2,\qquad
\mathrm{Ownership\mbox{-}HHI}_t=\sum_g S_{gt}^2.
\tag{A.1}\label{eq:review-o1-indices}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Averaging HHI across months}
\label{app:aggregation}
For a window $W$ of $n_W$ observed months, $\bar s_{g,W}$ is group $g$'s mean monthly share. The two summaries are
\begin{equation}
\underbrace{\sum_g \bar s_{g,W}^{\,2}}_{\text{HHI of average shares}},
\qquad
\underbrace{\frac{1}{n_W}\sum_{t\in W}\mathrm{HHI}_t}_{\text{average monthly HHI}}.
\tag{A.2}\label{eq:shared-aggregation}
\end{equation}
O2's exit contributions use the first. O1's period summaries and O2's observed consolidation changes use the second. Monthly observations receive equal weight.
