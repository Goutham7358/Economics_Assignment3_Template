\section{Limitations}
\begingroup
\color{black}
National concentration uses passenger shares, while route concentration uses scheduled flights. Scheduled services can differ from actual operations, and their shares do not account for aircraft size or occupancy. Airline-level route passenger data are unavailable, and missing competitor filings may overstate scheduled-flight shares. Entrants are observed in different seasons and some have limited route or funding records, which restricts comparisons across airlines. April 2020 is missing nationally, and missing AirAsia India records in 2017 overstate HHI. Rivals' unused capacity is only an overall estimate of spare capacity relative to IndiGo's traffic. It cannot establish actual passenger accommodation without matching seats to the required routes and dates.

The event comparisons are descriptive. Airlines declined before exiting, other changes overlap the comparison periods, and one indicator covers the entire COVID period from March 2020 to December 2021. Results also depend on ownership grouping, the nine-entrant sample and study-defined share and persistence thresholds. Entrant financial records differ in source, year and accounting basis, with some provisional, revised or press-reported figures, so financial comparisons are indicative. Only 20 months follow the mergers, and airlines still operating in July 2026 may have different eventual outcomes. CCI assesses city-pair markets rather than the national market, and some commitment thresholds are redacted. Without consistent fare or cost data, the results describe market structure and cannot establish pricing power or fare effects. Cournot markups are therefore illustrative.
