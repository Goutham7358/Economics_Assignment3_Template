\subsubsection{O1: Formulae and model specification}
\label{app:review-o1-formulae}
Shares, ownership mapping and the common indices follow Appendix~\ref{app:shared-calculations}. MHHI adds the partial-ownership adjustment:
\begin{equation}
\text{MHHI}_t = \text{Ownership-HHI}_t+\sum_{j}\sum_{k\neq j}\lambda_{jk}S_{jt}S_{kt},\qquad
\lambda_{jk}=\frac{\sum_i \gamma_{ij}\beta_{ik}}{\sum_i \gamma_{ij}\beta_{ij}} \tag{O1.1b}\label{eq:review-o1-mhhi}
\end{equation}
Here $\beta_{ik}$ is owner $i$'s stake in firm $k$ and $\gamma_{ik}$ its control share \parencite{obriensalop2000}. The regression is
\begin{equation}
\text{Index}_t = \alpha + \beta t + \sum_{k=1}^{4}\gamma_k\,\text{Post}_{kt} + \delta\,\text{COVID}_t + \sum_{m=2}^{12}\mu_m M_{mt} + \varepsilon_t \tag{O1.2}\label{eq:review-o1-regression}
\end{equation}
Here $\beta$ is the monthly trend, $\gamma_k$ the four permanent event steps, $\delta$ the COVID effect and $\mu_m$ the seasonal effects (January base). Dates and COVID window: Section~\ref{sec:method-o1}. Inference uses Newey--West errors with 12 lags \parencite{neweywest1987}; shifts are descriptive, not causal.
\begin{samepage}
For each of Brand HHI, Ownership HHI and MHHI, let $x_t$ be the change from the previous available observation. The modified score is
\begingroup
\renewcommand{\theHequation}{review-o1-jump}
\begin{equation}
M_t=\frac{0.6745\,[x_t-\operatorname{median}(x)]}{\operatorname{median}|x-\operatorname{median}(x)|}.
\tag{O1.3}\label{eq:review-o1-jump}
\end{equation}
\endgroup
\par\end{samepage}
The median and MAD use all observed changes \parencite{iglewicz1993outliers}; $|M_t|>3.5$ in any series flags either direction. Missing April 2020 makes May's change span March--May. The separately specified D1--D7 windows pass the nearby-jump check only if a flag lies within 40 days of the end-month; D1 and D6 fail. Window changes are end minus start; context and reversals are descriptive.
