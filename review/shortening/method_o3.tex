\label{sec:method-o3}
\textbf{O3.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Entrant growth and persistence.} Set $\tau=0$ at the first month with recorded passengers and number later months sequentially to compare entrants at the same stage. Record shares at $\tau=12,24,36$, peak share, traffic months and operating outcome. An entrant is \emph{meaningful} if its share reaches 1\% in any month and \emph{persistent} if it remains at or above 1\% for 24 consecutive months. Both cut-offs are study-defined. Airlines still flying are right-censored at July 2026.
    \item \textbf{Route presence.} Use each entrant's latest usable season before closure or merger, or its latest available season. Count departures using operating weekdays and service start/end dates, pooling both directions. Frequency share is the airline's departures divided by all recorded departures on that airport pair over the same full season, times 100. Sole recorded operator means 100\%. Dominant means at least 50\%. Keep monthly checks separate. Summer 2026 includes planned departures beyond July. Definitions and workings are in Appendices~\ref{app:o3-definitions} and~\ref{app:results-o3}.
    \item \textbf{Financial comparison.} Compare operating entrants' net profit or loss for FY2024-25 using the ministry's reported figures. Financial evidence helps assess sustainability, which national share growth alone cannot establish.
\end{itemize}
