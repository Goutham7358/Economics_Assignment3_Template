\subsection{Inputs, units and ownership mapping}
\label{app:o4-input-definitions}
National market-month observations use positive-passenger scheduled-domestic records from DGCA via \textcite{kubicek2026aviationstats}. Passengers are journeys, not unique travellers. Route observations use DGCA approved scheduled departures \parencite{dgcaDomesticSchedules}, pooling both directions of an airport pair; GOI and GOX remain distinct. The top 20 pairs and top-ten subset are fixed by total 2025 passenger journeys, not reranked monthly. ASK is seats times distance; RPK is revenue passengers times distance. Source capacity values are in thousands: divide by 1,000,000 for billions.

\textbf{Ownership convention.} Air India and Air India Express are combined throughout, as are Jet Airways and JetLite. Vistara and AirAsia India/AIX Connect also join Air India Group from January 2022; they are separate earlier. Other brands, including Alliance Air, remain separate.

\textbf{Periods and sample flags.} National: January 2015--July 2026, excluding missing April 2020. Routes: available April 2019--September 2026. Focus period: December 2024--July 2026. Top-ten membership selects fixed ranks 1--10. The buffer compares December 2024 with December 2025.

\subsection{Common measures}
\label{app:o4-measure-definitions}
Shares are percentages; $g$, $r$ and $t$ denote group, airport pair and month. Ranks $(1)$ and $(2)$ identify the two largest shares, reranked each observation; pp denotes percentage points. For brand shares $s_{it}$ and group shares $S_{gt}=\sum_{i\in g}s_{it}$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{Brand\mbox{-}HHI}_t=\sum_i s_{it}^2,\qquad
\mathrm{Ownership\mbox{-}HHI}_t=\sum_g S_{gt}^2.
\tag{A.1}\label{eq:review-o1-indices}
\end{equation}

Calculations precede rounding. Summaries equally weight retained observations; standard deviations use the sample formula and route quartiles use linear interpolation. HHI has a 0--10,000 scale. Missing values are not zero.

\subsection{Aggregation conventions}
For a window $W$ with $n_W$ observed months, distinguish the HHI of period-average shares from average monthly HHI:
\[
\bar s_{g,W}=\frac{1}{n_W}\sum_{t\in W}s_{gt},\qquad
\mathrm{HHI}(\bar s_W)=\sum_g\bar s_{g,W}^{\,2},\qquad
\overline{\mathrm{HHI}}_W=\frac{1}{n_W}\sum_{t\in W}\mathrm{HHI}_t.
\]
The difference $\overline{\mathrm{HHI}}_W-\mathrm{HHI}(\bar s_W)$ is the sum of share variances with denominator $n_W$. O2's exit contributions use $\mathrm{HHI}(\bar s_W)$; its observed Tata comparison and O1's period summaries use $\overline{\mathrm{HHI}}_W$. Monthly shares receive equal weight, rather than pooling window passengers.

Likewise, $10{,}000/\overline{\mathrm{HHI}}_W$ generally differs from mean monthly $N_t$: O2 reports the former and O1 summarises the latter. Route medians use retained pair-months, not pooled flights or monthly summaries. Entrant seasonal shares instead pool the season's departures. Missing observations are omitted, never set to zero.
