\section{Implications and Recommendations}
\label{sec:review-recommendations}
\begingroup
\color{black}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em,itemsep=8pt,parsep=0pt,topsep=6pt]
    \item \textbf{DGCA and CCI.} DGCA should publish monthly airline passenger counts, seats and actual departures by airport pair, together with dated records of ownership and minority stakes. Report Brand HHI, Ownership HHI and CR2 on a consistent basis, and monitor entrants' sustained passenger presence alongside the number of brands. Tables~\ref{tab:o1-regression}, \ref{tab:o3-summary} and~\ref{tab:o4-route-focus} show why ownership, entrant scale and market definition matter. CCI should use these records alongside airport-slot holdings and route overlaps in merger reviews, and publish compliance with route capacity commitments. These records would allow national and airport-pair concentration to be compared on the same passenger basis.

    \item \textbf{MoCA and airport operators.} Give smaller and newer airlines priority when reallocating an exiting airline's slots, while assessing whether their proposed services can be sustained. IndiGo gained the most passenger share after both exits, while SpiceJet's capacity expansion accompanied its receipt of vacated Jet slots (Table~\ref{tab:o2-absorption} and Appendix~\ref{app:results-o2}). For regional connectivity programmes such as UDAN, assess continued operations, passenger volumes and financial sustainability alongside route awards and commencements. TruJet's experience shows why a high local share and route awards alone do not establish durable service (Appendix~\ref{app:results-o3}).

    \item \textbf{Airlines, entrants and lenders.} Base expansion on achievable passenger volumes, route economics and committed funding. The entrant comparisons show that revenue growth and a large share of a small route did not consistently ensure profitability or survival (Tables~\ref{tab:o3-summary}--\ref{tab:o3-fin}). Airlines should also agree passenger-transfer procedures with MoCA and DGCA for a major IndiGo disruption, and test them against passenger demand and seats available on the affected routes and dates. The smaller aggregate rival capacity buffer in Table~\ref{tab:o4-capacity-buffer} supports this preparation, but does not measure how many disrupted passengers could actually be accommodated.
\end{itemize}
\endgroup
